Our Story

Founded by a Teacher Who Saw the Gap

Carmen Delgado spent 12 years in Orange County Public Schools watching bilingual kids fall through the cracks. Puente was born to catch them.

Our tutors are bilingual, credentialed, and from Conway, Azalea Park, and Meadow Woods -- the same communities we serve.
